## Innova and MEP Engineers Limited

### Context and role

Engineering products and services need clear communication for prospective clients. My work involved translating HVAC and fire protection information into accessible digital content, coordinating with engineering and sales colleagues, and supporting the company's digital presence.

**Timeline:** Marketing Executive, part-time, August 2023–June 2025; Digital Marketer, full-time, July 2025–August 2026.

### My responsibilities

- Developed and managed websites using WordPress, Elementor, and WooCommerce.
- Worked on navigation, page structure, and the user journey.
- Created digital content and visual assets using Canva and Figma.
- Coordinated with engineers to explain technical specifications in clear language.
- Used Google Analytics and campaign reporting to inform website and marketing work.
- Supported marketing coordination and business development objectives.

### What this work demonstrates

This project connects technical subject matter with customer-facing communication. It also combines website management, content design, stakeholder collaboration, and measurement within one business context.

### My responsibilities

### What this work demonstrates

### Evidence and measurement

[Read the documented ClypChip case study](https://github.com/assassinjahid/marketing-toolkit/blob/main/CLYPCHIP-CASE-STUDY.md), including six original creative compositions, June–September 2024 campaign exports, reporting CSVs and reproducible calculations.

## E-Learning and Earning Ltd

### Context and role

I worked with a training provider serving adult learners, including working professionals. My role combined practical digital marketing and teaching.

**Timeline:** Lead Trainer, Digital Marketing, January 2025; Digital Marketer, February–June 2025, with continuing contributions to training.

### My responsibilities

- Developed and implemented digital marketing campaigns.
- Worked across social media, SEO, content creation, and paid advertising.
- Supported lead generation and customer acquisition work.
- Analysed campaign performance to inform marketing decisions.
- Created original slide decks, lesson notes, and teaching materials.
- Delivered classes and mentored aspiring digital marketers.

### Available project material

My working archive includes seminar lead-form copy, branding reel scripts, content planning materials, and teaching resources. The seminar brief includes form wording, a confirmation message, and a call to action. These materials support a process-focused review; they do not by themselves prove campaign performance.

### Process review example

A seminar lead-generation workflow needs a consistent city, audience, offer, form, and follow-up message. A practical quality check is to compare location references across the advertisement, form title, introductory copy, and venue details before launch.

**Recommended measurement framework:** track spend, valid registrations, attendance, and enrolments separately. Report cost per valid registration and cost per enrolment only when the corresponding records are available. This framework is a portfolio recommendation, not a statement of historical results.

### What this work demonstrates

This work links marketing communication with learner needs. The teaching portfolio provides a complementary view of my ability to explain tools and guide adults through practical tasks.
